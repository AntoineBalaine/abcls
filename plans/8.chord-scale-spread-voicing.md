## Outline

- Phase 1 adds `buildScaleSpreadVoicing` to `parse/music-theory/harmonization.ts`. It starts from the chord scale close voicing of plan 7 and pushes the voices down by octaves, one at a time, until a limit stops it.
- Phase 2 adds the voicing type `scaleSpread` to `voiceChord`, the key `g S` of the transform mode, the option of the command line and the row of the documentation table.

Each phase carries its tests, with example cases and properties.

## Context

The old spread voicing builds its middle voices from the tables `getArrangements4`, `getArrangements5` and `getArrangements6`. Because the tables encode the old tension rule and the roles by case, they are hard to extend. The close voicing of plan 7 already has a consistent chord scale, so the spread is derived from it.

Three earlier proposals were rejected, and the reasons shape the design:

- A close block with a far root added below is not a spread, because the intervals must widen gradually toward the bottom.
- A table of gaps is invented, because the gaps must follow from the chord and not from numbers.
- Assigning the roles of the Berklee text to voices and placing each at the nearest allowed note gave bad results, because the closest intervals must be in the upper voices.

The accepted idea is a loop that pushes the voices to the bottom by octaves. For Cmaj7 under E5 with five voices, the close voicing E C B A G becomes E B A G C (the root goes to the bottom), then E A G B C (B drops and pushes the root down), and the next step is refused by the low interval limits.

## Decisions already taken

- The new voicing is a new `VoicingType`, `scaleSpread`, reachable with `g S`. The old spread stays on `g s`.
- The loop keeps the last valid step. A position that is best on its own may join the next chord badly, so the voice leading is left for later.
- The bass is always the root.
- The work is done on the current branch `modal-keymap`.

## The algorithm

```lua
close = buildScaleCloseVoicing(rootPosChord, tensions, leadMidi, voiceCount)
others = the voices of close under the lead, from the top to the bottom

if no voice of others has the pitch class of the root
  remove the lowest tension from others, or else the lowest fifth        -- room for the bass
else
  remove the lowest voice that has the pitch class of the root           -- it becomes the bass

unmoved = others
bass = the nearest root below the lowest unmoved voice
stack = {bass}                                -- the moved voices, from the top to the bottom
state = {lead, unmoved, stack}
if state is not valid then return null

repeat
  if unmoved has fewer than 2 voices then stop
  v = the first voice of unmoved                -- the voice right under the lead
  unmoved = the rest
  v = the nearest occurrence of v below the lowest unmoved voice
  shift the whole stack down by octaves until its top voice lies below v
  stack = {v} + stack
  if the new state is not valid then stop, and keep the previous state
```

A state is valid when:

- the gap between two adjacent voices above the bass is at most an octave (`MAX_UPPER_GAP`);
- the gap between the bass and the voice above it is at most a 10th (16 semitones);
- the bass respects `LOW_INTERVAL_LIMITS` for the interval between the bass and the voice above it.

## Phase 1: the builder

File: `parse/music-theory/harmonization.ts`, exported from `parse/music-theory/index.ts`.

New exports:

- `MAX_BASS_GAP` (number, 16).
- `SpreadState` (interface with `lead: VoicedNote`, `unmoved: VoicedNote[]` and `stack: VoicedNote[]`; `VoicedNote` is defined in the same file).
- `spreadStateVoices(state: SpreadState): VoicedNote[]` (the voices from the lowest to the highest).
- `isSpreadStateValid(voices: VoicedNote[]): boolean`.
- `moveNextSpreadVoice(state: SpreadState): SpreadState | null`.
- `pickVoiceToRemoveForBass(others: VoicedNote[]): number` (the index, or -1).
- `buildScaleSpreadVoicing(rootPosChord: VoicedNote[], tensions: Map<9 | 11 | 13, VoicedNote>, leadMidi: number, voiceCount: number): VoicedNote[] | null`.

Tests, in `parse/tests/chordScaleVoicing.spec.ts`:

- Example cases: Cmaj7 under E5 with five voices gives E A G B C, with the midi values 76, 69, 67, 59 and 48; Cmaj7 under E5 with four voices gives E A B C; a lead that is not in the chord scale returns null.
- Properties: the voicing has exactly `voiceCount` voices; the lead is on top; the lowest voice is the root and is the only voice below the others that has the root pitch class, except when the lead is the root; the voices are strictly ascending; the gaps respect the three limits of a valid state; every pitch class belongs to the chord scale or is the root.
